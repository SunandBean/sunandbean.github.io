Race Information

Race calendars and where to watch them, for nine series in one place.

  • F1, WEC, WRC, NASCAR Cup, INDYCAR, IMSA, GT World Challenge Europe, SuperRace, Hyundai N Festival
  • Filter by viewing market (US, KR, global) and by your timezone
  • Add any race to Google or Apple Calendar; every entry links its official source

Turns an arXiv paper into a narrated, fact-checked explainer video.

  • Three.js scenes, local Kokoro text-to-speech, ffmpeg
  • Narration in English (Kokoro) or Korean (Supertonic)

Seven models on one p100a

Image, video and music models ported to a single Tenstorrent Blackhole p100a card.

  • FLUX.2 klein, Z-Image-Turbo, HiDream-O1, Qwen-Image-Edit, Wan 2.2, ACE-Step 1.5, DiffRhythm 1.2
  • A 1024×1024 Z-Image-Turbo image in 7.76 s; a 30-second ACE-Step song in 3.3 s
  • Apache-2.0 code, weights on Hugging Face

Big language models on my desk

Two Qwen mixture-of-experts models served from the same card, behind OpenAI- and Anthropic-compatible APIs.

  • Qwen3.6-35B-A3B: every weight on the card, about 82 to 106 tokens/s for one user
  • Qwen3.8-Flash-Next (125B): card plus host CPU, about 10 to 17 tokens/s
  • Works as a backend for Claude Code and OpenAI clients
